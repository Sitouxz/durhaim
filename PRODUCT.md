# DURHAIM public storefront

DURHAIM is an Indonesian tactical-equipment brand built around durability, hard impact, and modular use. The public site must communicate field readiness through decisive product photography, direct language, and an unembellished black, white, and grey interface.

## Jobs the storefront must do

1. Let a customer understand the equipment categories and product series quickly.
2. Let a buyer inspect a product, its colorway, ordered gallery, specifications, and regional purchasing information.
3. Let an owner verify a serial number manually or by scanning a QR code, then open its certificate.
4. Preserve contact, WhatsApp ordering, newsletter, localization, SEO, and regional-pricing flows.

## Product principles

- Figma desktop artboards are the visual source of truth.
- Tactic Sans and the supplied photographic assets are brand requirements, not optional decoration.
- Public pages are monochrome and sharp. Admin pages retain the existing operational interface.
- Missing commercial data is omitted cleanly; it is never replaced with invented values.
- Accessibility, corrected copy, and working retained features take precedence over reproducing a design defect.
- Responsive layouts preserve the Figma composition and reading order from 320px through wide desktop.

## Success criteria

- All public routes use the DURHAIM storefront system with no runtime Figma or Drive dependency.
- Catalogue discovery, verification, localization, regional pricing, WhatsApp, newsletter, and SEO remain functional.
- Reference captures at 1920px stay within a 1% changed-pixel threshold after documented masks for retained utilities.
- Keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, and WCAG AA contrast are supported.
